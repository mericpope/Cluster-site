The service

Cluster is a personal finance and travel-tracking app that helps you keep track of your own accounts, cards, trips, documents, and residency status across countries and currencies. Six modules — Countries, Cards, Accounts, Wealth, Spend, and Documents — work together to give you a single view of your cross-border finances.

Cluster does not connect to your bank accounts or move money on your behalf. Financial data is either entered manually or imported by you via CSV. Cluster is a record-keeping and visibility tool, not a bank, broker, payment processor, or financial institution.
